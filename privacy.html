Drumless Studio Licence Privacy

Privacy Policy — Drumless Studio

Publisher: TristanDumesny Application: Drumless Studio Contact: drumlessstudio@gmail.com Last updated: 4 August 2026

Summary

Drumless Studio processes your audio locally on your device. The App does not upload your audio to us and does not include our own analytics, advertising or tracking system.

Some optional network features involve Microsoft or third-party download providers. Those providers may receive standard connection information, such as your IP address, when their services are used. If you contact us for support, we receive the information you choose to send.

1. Audio and project files

Audio files you open in Drumless Studio, and files the App creates from them, including separated stems, mixes, click tracks and analysis data, are processed on your device.

  • The App does not upload your audio to us for processing.
  • We do not remotely access, monitor, hear or store your local projects.
  • Project and output files are written to locations on your computer, including

locations you select and any default project folder used by the App.

  • Using the App's project-deletion function removes files managed by that project

where technically possible. It does not remove copies you exported, moved or backed up elsewhere.

We cannot recover local files that you delete. Keep backups of important files.

2. Information sent to the Publisher

The App does not operate a Drumless Studio user-account system and does not send us analytics, advertising identifiers or telemetry.

If you contact us by email or otherwise request support, we may receive:

  • your name and email address;
  • the contents of your message;
  • device, App-version or diagnostic details you choose to provide; and
  • files or screenshots you choose to attach.

Do not send copyrighted or sensitive audio unless it is necessary and you have the right to send it. We use support information only to respond, troubleshoot, protect the App and keep reasonable business records. We retain it only for as long as reasonably needed for those purposes or as required by law.

3. Microsoft Store information

Drumless Studio is distributed through the Microsoft Store. Microsoft may process personal, account, device, licence, payment and transaction information when you view, install, purchase, update or manage the App.

Microsoft handles that information under its own privacy statement: https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement

We may receive Store reports such as install, acquisition, sales, refund and subscription figures. Depending on the report and Microsoft settings, this may be aggregated or otherwise limited. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

The App may ask Windows and the Microsoft Store on your device whether a valid licence or entitlement exists. That process is provided by Microsoft.

4. Network access and model downloads

The App may access the internet to:

  • verify or refresh a Microsoft Store licence or entitlement;
  • obtain App updates through the Microsoft Store; and
  • download AI model files or related resources needed for optional features.

The App does not include your audio in those requests. However, Microsoft and third-party model-hosting providers may receive standard technical information needed to provide the connection, such as IP address, request time, requested file, network headers and basic device or software information.

Third-party model sources and relevant licence information should be identified in the App's Third-Party Notices. Those providers process connection information under their own privacy terms.

5. Sharing and sale of information

We do not sell personal information and do not share your local audio or project files.

Information may be handled by service providers only where needed to provide a service you request, including:

  • Microsoft, for Store distribution, licensing, purchases and updates;
  • Google, where you contact our Gmail support address; and
  • third-party model-hosting providers, when the App downloads an optional model.

We may also disclose information where required by law or reasonably necessary to protect legal rights, users or the security of the App.

6. Overseas processing

Microsoft, Google and model-hosting providers may process information in countries outside Australia. Their locations and safeguards are governed by their own privacy terms and infrastructure.

7. Children

Drumless Studio is not specifically directed at children. The App does not knowingly operate a system for collecting children's personal information. Microsoft account, purchase and parental-consent requirements may apply through the Microsoft Store.

8. Your rights

Your privacy rights depend on the laws that apply to you and the organisation holding the information.

Because we do not receive your local audio or projects, we cannot access, correct, export or delete those files for you. You can manage them directly on your device.

For support information that we hold, you may contact us to request access, correction or deletion. We will respond as required by applicable law, including the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) where it applies.

For information held by Microsoft, use your Microsoft account privacy controls: https://account.microsoft.com/privacy

9. Security

The security of local audio and project files depends mainly on your device and the locations where you save them. Keep Windows and the App updated, use a strong device password, and consider disk encryption for sensitive files.

We use reasonable measures to protect support information we receive, but no email or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when the App, its network features or applicable laws change. The current version will be made available through the App or the Microsoft Store listing. Material changes will be notified through the App or Store listing.
