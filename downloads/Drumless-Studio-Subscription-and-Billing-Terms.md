## 3. What an active subscription includes

While the subscription is active, you may create unlimited new complete-song
projects, subject to ordinary device, storage, file-format and processing limits.
"Unlimited" means there is no publisher-imposed monthly project, credit or
minute quota. It does not mean simultaneous or instantaneous processing.

An active subscription includes complete-song stem separation, full playback,
click-track creation and editing, mix export and stem export for projects
processed or upgraded while the entitlement is active.

Automated separation and click detection results vary between recordings and
are not guaranteed to be perfect.

## 4. Access after a subscription ends

A project completed or upgraded to full access while a subscription is active
receives a local full-access grant. That local project remains fully playable
and exportable on the device after the subscription ends.

Subscription expiry does not unlock new projects and does not upgrade previews
that were never completed while an entitlement was active. Those new or locked
projects remain subject to the 60-second preview until another entitlement is
active.

Permanent project access depends on retaining the local project data. Drumless
Studio is not a cloud storage or backup service. Keep backups of important
projects and exports. Reinstalling Windows, uninstalling the App, deleting a
project or losing local files may remove data that the Publisher cannot recover.
